# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Setu Gateway community welcoming, safe, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and help others learn.
- Respect privacy; never share private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized language, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may warn, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially where possible.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles.
