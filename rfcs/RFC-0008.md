# RFC-0008: Database Architecture

- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

PostgreSQL is the source of truth. Use UUID primary keys, UTC timestamps, foreign keys, explicit tenant ownership, and soft deletion for important business resources. Use JSONB only for flexible metadata and plugin configuration. Domain tables cover identity, providers, gateway execution, routing, security, billing, analytics, events, plugins, prompts, cache, benchmarks, Time Machine, feature flags, workflows, and recommendations.

Separate logical OLTP and OLAP concerns from the beginning. PostgreSQL initially serves both; analytical workloads may move to ClickHouse or another warehouse later behind stable data contracts. Event/outbox processing makes analytics, billing, webhooks, and audit consumers reliable.

## Approved future schema

Feature flags, reserved workflow state, model performance history, and recommendation records are included without forcing early implementation.
