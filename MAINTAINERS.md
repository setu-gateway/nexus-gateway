## Becoming a maintainer

Start by contributing per [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). There's no fixed
tenure requirement - what matters is a track record of accepted PRs, thoughtful
reviews of others' work, and good judgment on the trade-offs specific to this
codebase (tenant isolation, provider-adapter contracts, backward compatibility on
the OpenAI-compatible surface). Maintainers propose new maintainers; see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the confirmation process.
