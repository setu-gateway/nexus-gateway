# The bridge between your apps and every LLM

**Setu** (सेतु) means "bridge." Setu Gateway sits between your applications and OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Ollama, Groq, and more — one OpenAI-compatible API, with capability-aware routing, health-based fallback, and zero-trust security by default.
